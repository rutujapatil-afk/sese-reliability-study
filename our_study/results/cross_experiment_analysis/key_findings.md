# SeSE Cross-Experiment Findings

This document summarizes the current experimental evidence generated by our independent robustness study.

## Findings

- Edge-weight noise level 0.05 produced a mean relative entropy change of -0.1120.
- Edge-weight noise level 0.10 produced a mean relative entropy change of -0.1766.
- Edge-weight noise level 0.20 produced a mean relative entropy change of -0.2661.
- Edge dropout level 0.05 produced a mean relative entropy change of -0.0000.
- Edge dropout level 0.10 produced a mean relative entropy change of 0.0084.
- Edge dropout level 0.20 produced a mean relative entropy change of 0.0481.
- Structural entropy varied across semantic/reasoning complexity levels.
  - contradictory_fabricated: mean structural entropy -1.809677
  - explanatory: mean structural entropy -1.386728
  - multi_claim: mean structural entropy -1.588609
  - reasoning_heavy: mean structural entropy -1.650362
  - simple_factual: mean structural entropy -1.440550
- Confident failures were observed in: capital_france, planet.
- Failure-mechanism analysis measured incorrect fraction alongside graph density, cluster imbalance, edge weights, and structural entropy.
- Threshold sensitivity results are available for comparison with the perturbation and complexity studies.
- Score-stability results are available for assessing whether uncertainty scores vary across repeated sampling.
